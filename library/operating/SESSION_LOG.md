# Session log

**Purpose:** Chronological record of significant session events.
**Owner:** <owner>
**Last updated:** 2026-01-01

---

Append-only. Each entry gets a heading, a severity line, and the
reasoning — not just the outcome.

The format:

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM — Short title

**Severity:** what broke and how badly, or "routine".

**What happened:** the facts.

**Why it mattered:** the decision or lesson that came out of it.
```

## YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM — Example entry

**Severity:** routine.

**What happened:** Something worth remembering happened.

**Why it mattered:** The reason this entry exists. If the only thing
worth recording is "something happened," it wasn't worth recording.
